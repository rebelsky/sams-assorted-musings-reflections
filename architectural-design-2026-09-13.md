Recently, a friend and I were talking about Grinnell's Civic Engagement Pavilion. They said something like,

> I don't understand the design. If you arrive late, you have to walk in from the front and pass by the speakers. If you must leave early, you also have to walk in front of everyone. And, G-d forbid, if you have to go to the bathroom, the bathrooms are directly behind the speakers; everyone knows what you're doing. Why would anyone design it that way?

I thought for a moment and gave what another friend might call a "Sammish answer".

> While the building was being designed, the lead architect was experiencing significant public humiliation. Clearly, they wanted others to have a similar experience.

That's all I have to say about that building, at least for today.
