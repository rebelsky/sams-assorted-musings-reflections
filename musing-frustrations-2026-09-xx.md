Back when I started musing, I managed to muse (or rant) daily. For the past few years, I've been less persistent, perhaps posting once a week. This semester, I decided to be a bit more persistent, and hoped that I could post daily. I failed. Worse yet, I've found that I am failing to predict the length of my musings. What I thought would be [a few comments on the new timetable](new-timetable-2026-09-09) took me about two hours, possibly because my muse compelled me to insert more of the backstory [1]. I thought writing about [early graduation](midyear-graduates-2026Fa) [2,3] would be quick. Another two hours down the drain.

On the one hand, I don't mind the time. I like writing. More specifically, I like playing with ideas, exploring how to express those ideas, and having to do some background "research" as I work through things. I learn as I write! Unfortunately, I don't always have two hours to spare. Hence, I try to find things that won't take me too much time, but will permit me to post daily. My mediocre [joke about the Civic Engagement Pavillion](architectural-design-2026-09-13) is one such example. However, I'd prefer a short musing that isn't quite so sort. A few paragraphs that teach, inform, give me a chance to learn, something like that. I hope I can find some soon!
