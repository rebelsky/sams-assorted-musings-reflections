Last week [1], on the day before classes, I attended multiple sessions of the annual Grinnell Colloquium [2]. The Grinnell Colloquium, for those not familiar with it, is a day of talks about various issues pertaining to the state of the College [3], its finances, and plans for the coming year. I particularly appreciate hearing about the finances of the College; we had a multi-year gap in those presentations after Kate resigned, and I'm glad to see them return.

This year, there were four topics: Finances of the College, Grinnell College in the Age of Generative AI, Declarations of Independence [4], and Evolving the Honor G. I attended three of the four. Since not everyone attends, I thought I'd share some of what I heard.

Perhaps I should start with the most terrifying part, at least to me. After our Chief Financial Officer gave the opening talk, she sat down in front of me, introduced herself, and said, "You're Sam Rebelsky. I've heard a lot about you." What do people say to our top-level administrators about me? Also concerning: In the Honor G section, President Harris credited me [6] for the Honor G revamp.
